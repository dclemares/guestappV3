Form redesign — 5 directions

All five drop the card boxes and use the summary-style section headers (icon + uppercase title). They differ in how fields and section rhythm are handled. Open each, then tell me which to iterate on.

1Hairline classic

Full-width hairline between sections, underline fields. The most literal merge of the summary header + the v2-minimal no-box style.

 2Eyebrow accent bar

No dividers — each header is an uppercase eyebrow with a short blue accent bar (the Vela eyebrow language). Underline fields, airy rhythm.

 3Soft filled

Tinted icon-chip headers and borderless soft-filled inputs. Friendlier and softer than pure underlines, still box-free.

 4Two-column ledger

Section title sits in a sticky left column, fields on the right. Settings-page feel, very scannable.

 5Summary rows

Single-column label-left rows with hairlines — directly echoes the guest-summary list. Values align right like an editable summary.
